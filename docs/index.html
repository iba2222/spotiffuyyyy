Install from your phone — SideStore / AltStore

  1. Install SideStore (or AltStore) on the device and get it signed in with your Apple ID.
  2. Open Sources → + and paste the address below.
  3. Tap Iayxify → Install. SideStore signs the build with your account on the device.
https://iba2222.github.io/spotiffuyyyy/source.json

Install from a computer — Sideloadly

  1. Download the IPA above and install Sideloadly (Windows or macOS).
  2. Connect the iPhone by USB and drag the IPA into Sideloadly.
  3. Enter your Apple ID, start, then trust the developer certificate in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.

The IPA in Releases is an unsigned build of patched Spotify. iOS will only run it after it has been signed with your own Apple ID — SideStore/AltStore do that on the device, Sideloadly does it on your computer.
